Nyaya-Sarathi: Multi-Agent Legal Assistance System

Overview Link to heading

🏆 Winner — Epochon 2.0 Agentic AI Hackathon.

Nyaya-Sarathi is a multi-agent grievance redressal and legal assistance system designed to make legal guidance and complaint filing genuinely accessible to ordinary citizens. Built around an agentic AI architecture, it pairs rigorous legal reasoning with a deliberately transparent, auditable decision pipeline.

Key Features Link to heading

  • Adversarial multi-agent deliberation with a final arbiter that resolves disagreements between specialised agents.
  • RAG-grounded legal reasoning with white-box logic — every conclusion is tied back to retrieved legal context.
  • Intent detection that distinguishes between users seeking legal guidance and users who want to file a formal complaint.
  • Automated complaint report generation producing structured, submission-ready documents.
  • IVR-based agent layer (proposed): users can interact with the system via a phone call — no internet required — significantly improving accessibility for underserved populations.

Impact Link to heading

  • Awarded first prize at Epochon 2.0.
  • Demonstrates a practical pattern for building AI systems where reasoning transparency is a first-class concern, not an afterthought.
  • Pushes accessibility forward by combining LLM reasoning with traditional telephony (IVR).
